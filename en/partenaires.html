Crochet designer · Antibes

Tigre & Mouette

Tigre & Mouette is a crochet studio based in Antibes, on the French Riviera. Tops, bikinis and accessories are entirely handcrafted, stitch by stitch, in small batches — no machines, no outsourcing.

The studio stands by a clear charter: natural or recycled materials to limit environmental impact, and a deliberate choice to support local artisans rather than mass production. Cotton, worked for its softness and durability, remains its material of choice.

Each piece is unique and made to measure: the color, pattern and finishes are chosen for each order, placed directly by message on Instagram.

We spotlight Tigre & Mouette because we share the same philosophy: handmade, local, and time well spent over mass production. The creations are now also available in-store, in Antibes.
