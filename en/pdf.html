FAQ

PDF or app?

What's the difference between the PDF and the app?
The PDF is a static, printable version of the program: perfect if you prefer paper or don't want a subscription. The app goes much further: progress tracking, AI coach, meal scan, a Camera Coach that counts your reps, connected watch, reminders… The 28-day training content is the same — it's the experience around it that changes.
Is it a subscription?
No. The PDF is a one-time purchase of €29: you pay once, you download, it's yours forever. No renewal, no recurring charge.
How do I receive my PDF?
Immediately after Stripe payment, the download link appears and is emailed to you. If you can't find it, write to us via the contact page and we'll resend it.
Is the PDF suitable for beginners?
Yes. Each program starts gently in week 1 and gradually builds intensity. The exercises are illustrated and explained step by step. KlynShape without equipment is the most accessible to start with.
I bought a PDF — can I switch to the app?
Of course — the app offers a 3-day free trial, no commitment. You can try it whenever you like from the download page.

Want the complete coach?

Real-time tracking, AI coach, meal scan, Camera Coach, connected watch — it's all in the KLYN app. 3-day free trial, no commitment.
